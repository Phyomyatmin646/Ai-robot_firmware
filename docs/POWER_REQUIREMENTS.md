# Power Architecture & Safety Requirements

## 1. Safety Warning Regarding Power Input

> [!CAUTION]
> **DO NOT connect an unregulated 12V supply directly to the ESP32-S3 module or its 3.3V / 5V GPIO header pins!**
> The ESP32-S3 and all connected digital peripherals (INMP441, ILI9341 logic, Touch sensor, PN532 logic) operate strictly at **3.3V logic level**. Applying higher voltages will destroy the microcontroller immediately.

---

## 2. Power Adapter & V2775 Expansion Board Voltage Regulation

The system uses an **Adjustable DC Adapter (3V–12V, 2A)**.

### 2.1 Expansion Board V2775 On-Board Regulators (Confirmed from PCB Inspection)
Inspection of the V2775 Expansion Board PCB reveals:
1. **DC Barrel Jack (Center Positive `5.5mm x 2.1mm`):**
   - Directly feeds an on-board switching buck converter / linear regulator circuit.
   - Identified components:
     - `78M05` 5V linear regulator / LDO (Vin max ~35V, Vout = 5.0V, Iout up to 500mA).
     - `2R2` step-down inductor & high-efficiency switching regulator IC (capable of supplying 5V at up to 2A to handle the MAX98357A amplifier and display backlight without overheating).
     - Two 100µF 16V filter electrolytic capacitors.
2. **On-Board Power Distribution:**
   - **5V Rail:** Powered from the buck/linear output, feeds the ESP32-S3 DevKit 5V input pin and the dedicated 5V headers (red pin rows).
   - **3.3V Rail:** Generated by the high-current LDO on the ESP32-S3 DevKit board (and reinforced by the expansion board), feeding all 3.3V yellow/red sensor headers.

### 2.2 Recommended Adapter Setting
- **Recommended DC Adapter Voltage:** **Set adapter to 7.5V or 9.0V DC** (Do NOT set to 12V unless necessary, to minimize thermal stress on the 78M05; do NOT set below 6.5V if feeding the DC barrel jack, to avoid drop-out voltage).
- **Alternative (Direct 5V):** If using a dedicated 5V 2A regulated USB-C adapter connected to the ESP32 DevKit USB port, set the DC adapter to 5V only if connecting directly to a dedicated 5V rail.

## 3. Power Budget Analysis

| Subsystem / Component | Voltage Rail | Typical Current | Peak Current | Power (Peak) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **ESP32-S3 N16R8 (CPU 240MHz + Wi-Fi TX)** | 3.3V | 120 mA | 450 mA | ~1.5 W |
| **ILI9341 2.8" LCD Screen & Logic** | 3.3V | 25 mA | 40 mA | 0.13 W |
| **ILI9341 LED Backlight (100% PWM)** | 3.3V / 5V | 60 mA | 80 mA | 0.26 W |
| **INMP441 MEMS Microphone** | 3.3V | 1.4 mA | 2 mA | < 0.01 W |
| **PN532 RFID Reader (Active field)** | 3.3V / 5V | 80 mA | 150 mA | 0.75 W |
| **TTP223 Touch Sensor** | 3.3V | 0.01 mA | 0.05 mA | < 0.001 W |
| **MAX98357A + 8Ω 2W Speaker (Peak playback)** | 5.0V | 150 mA | 550 mA | 2.75 W |
| **Total Worst-Case Simultaneous Peak** | — | — | **~1.3 A** | **~5.4 W** |

The 2A power adapter provides **more than 50% safety margin** over the 1.3A worst-case peak current draw.

## 4. Speaker Protection & Amplifier Limiting

The connected speaker is an **8Ω 2W rated driver**.
The MAX98357A can deliver up to 3.2W into 4Ω at 5V, or ~1.8W into 8Ω at 5V with 10% THD (clipping).

To protect the 2W speaker from voice-coil overheating, thermal distortion, or mechanical tearing:
1. **Gain Configuration:** Set the MAX98357A `GAIN` pin to GND (sets fixed 9 dB voltage gain, avoiding excessive amplification).
2. **Firmware Digital Limiter:**
   - Digital volume control in firmware maps 0–100% linearly to digital multipliers with a soft-knee saturation curve.
   - Peak digital output is clamped at -1.5 dBFS to prevent DAC digital clipping.
3. **Power-On Anti-Pop:**
   - Keep MAX98357A `SD_MODE` held low (or clear I2S DMA buffers with zeroes) during boot until the I2S audio clock PLL stabilizes.
